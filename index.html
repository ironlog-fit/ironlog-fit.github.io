About

IronLog is an independent, self-hosted personal tracker. It was inspired by the feature set of fitness apps like EliteEngine (workout logs, habit tracking, macro tracking, XP ranks, an AI coach) but shares no code, design assets or branding with them, and isn't affiliated with School of Elites or Teekay.
